## Study-specific bounded notes

The frozen Ridge implementation uses lambda=10; training-only mean/std with ddof=0; zero-variance scale is set to 1; intercept is unpenalized. The prediction target is log(V_1h24), with no smearing correction.
Reference variance is 0.0002824090500991152; entry-only risk multiplier q=min(1,sqrt(reference/prediction)). In the 2025 strategies (95 + 155 + 41 = 291 entries), every entry q=1 and trades/NAV CSVs are byte-equal to q=1. This does not mean every forecast was below reference: paired 2025 forecasts include 6 ETH and 50 SOL origins above reference, not at these entry times.
