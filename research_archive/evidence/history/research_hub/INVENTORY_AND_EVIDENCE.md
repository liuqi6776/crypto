## Interpretation rules

- `EXPLORATORY_UNVERIFIED` and `LEGACY_RESEARCH` are evidence labels, not grades. `INVALIDATED` means the cited result or inference must not be used as valid evidence in its current form.
- Three seeds over shared history are not three independent market samples. Overlapping origins, candidate rows and ledgers are not independent samples. A count without a defensible independent sample unit is explicitly marked unknown or overlapping.
- Prediction metrics, trade-level results and account-level results are distinct units. A sum of independent case ledgers is descriptive and is not a combined-account return.
- Existing reports were not re-audited or regenerated for this hub. References with `NOT_VERIFIED_PERMISSION_DENIED` identify a known access boundary; no attempt was made to read around it.
